關於陳思翰About Szu-Han Chen

陳思翰為國立陽明交通大學醫學系學生（MD candidate），並於哈佛大學陳曾熙公共衛生學院參與研究合作。研究聚焦心血管與營養流行病學，包括心臟衰竭、高血壓、鈉攝取與心血管代謝風險。Szu-Han Chen is a medical student (MD candidate) at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University and a research collaborator at Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health. His research focuses on cardiovascular and nutritional epidemiology, including heart failure, hypertension, sodium intake, and cardiometabolic risk.

Current Work

目前的學術工作Current academic work

目前的研究連結臨床醫學、族群健康與流行病學方法，使用世代研究、統合分析、因果推論與中介分析等方法，探討可轉化為預防與風險分層的證據。His current work connects clinical medicine, population health, and epidemiologic methods, using cohort studies, evidence synthesis, causal inference, and mediation analysis to study evidence that can inform prevention and risk stratification.

Research Focus

研究焦點Research focus

主要主題包括心血管代謝健康與心臟衰竭、高血壓與鈉攝取、營養暴露與代謝體學，以及健康照護與身心障礙健康公平。Primary topics include cardiometabolic health and heart failure; hypertension and sodium intake; nutritional exposures and metabolomics; and health services and disability health equity.
