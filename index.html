心血管與營養流行病學，從族群資料到臨床照護。Cardiovascular and nutritional epidemiology, from population data to clinical care.

這個網站整理我的研究興趣、進行中的計畫、學術發表與寫作。核心問題很直接：哪些人會在什麼條件下承受較高的心血管、腎臟與代謝風險，我們又該如何用資料設計更公平、更有效的預防策略？This site collects my research interests, ongoing projects, publications, and writing. The core question is simple: who carries higher cardiovascular, kidney, and metabolic risk, under what conditions, and how can we use data to design fairer and more effective prevention?
