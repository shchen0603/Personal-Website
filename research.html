Direction 01

心血管代謝健康與心臟衰竭Cardiometabolic Health & Heart Failure

肥胖、中央型脂肪、發炎、CKM 分期、心肌應變與心臟衰竭風險。Obesity, central adiposity, inflammation, CKM staging, myocardial strain, and heart failure risk.

這個方向關注心血管、腎臟與代謝系統如何共同塑造心臟衰竭風險，並嘗試將族群資料中的風險訊號轉化為更早期的預防與分層照護線索。This direction examines how the cardiovascular, kidney, and metabolic systems jointly shape heart failure risk, and how risk signals in population data can be translated into earlier prevention and risk-stratified care.

研究轉譯紀錄：相關的 AHA Newsroom 報導（腹部脂肪、發炎與心臟衰竭風險）在 AHA 最終成效報告中累積 217 則報導、558,935,079 次曝光。Research translation note: the related AHA Newsroom release on abdominal adiposity, inflammation, and heart failure risk was tracked in AHA's final coverage report as 217 stories and 558,935,079 impressions.

Direction 02

高血壓、鈉攝取與血管風險Hypertension, Sodium, and Vascular Risk

鹽敏感性、個人化血壓–鈉反應、夜間高血壓與血流動力學。Salt sensitivity, personalized BP-sodium response, nocturnal hypertension, and hemodynamics.

這個方向聚焦血壓調控與血管風險的異質性，特別是不同族群對鈉攝取與血壓反應的差異，以及夜間血壓與血流動力學在風險評估中的角色。This direction focuses on heterogeneity in blood pressure regulation and vascular risk—particularly how different populations respond to sodium intake, and the role of nocturnal blood pressure and hemodynamics in risk assessment.

Direction 03

營養、代謝體學與慢性病Nutrition, Metabolomics, and Chronic Disease

飲食型態、超加工食品分類、代謝體特徵與心血管代謝結局。Dietary patterns, UPF subclassification, metabolomic profiling, and cardiometabolic outcomes.

這個方向整合飲食型態、超加工食品分類、代謝體特徵與慢性病結局，理解營養暴露如何透過生物路徑影響長期心血管與代謝健康。This direction integrates dietary patterns, ultra-processed food subclassification, metabolomic signatures, and chronic disease outcomes to understand how nutritional exposures shape long-term cardiovascular and metabolic health through biological pathways.
