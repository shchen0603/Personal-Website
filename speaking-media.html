Short Bio

短版講者介紹Short speaker bio

陳思翰為國立陽明交通大學醫學系學生（MD candidate）及哈佛大學陳曾熙公共衛生學院研究合作者，研究聚焦心血管與營養流行病學。他曾受邀分享醫學生研究訓練與實證醫學，並於國內外學術會議報告心血管代謝、高血壓與營養相關研究。Szu-Han Chen is a medical student (MD candidate) at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University and a research collaborator at Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health. His work focuses on cardiovascular and nutritional epidemiology. He has given invited talks on medical student research training and evidence-based medicine, and has presented cardiometabolic, hypertension, and nutrition research at scientific meetings.
