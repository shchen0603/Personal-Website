How to invite

怎麼邀請How to invite me

來信時附上下面幾項資訊，我可以比較快回覆。Including the following in your email helps me reply quickly.

  • 主辦單位與場合Organizer and occasion 學會年會、院內演講、實驗室會議或 journal club。Annual meeting, hospital grand rounds, lab meeting or journal club.
  • 聽眾背景Audience 臨床醫師、研究人員、學生，或是跨領域的聽眾。Clinicians, researchers, students, or a mixed audience.
  • 希望的主題與時長Topic and length 可以指定上面的主題，也可以提出想討論的研究問題。Pick a topic above, or propose a research question you would like discussed.
  • 日期與語言Date and language 實體或線上、中文或英文。In person or online, Mandarin or English.

研究合作的討論也歡迎來信，請見聯絡頁的說明。Enquiries about research collaboration are also welcome; see the contact page.
