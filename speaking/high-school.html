For high-school teachers

給高中老師：把醫學研究帶進課堂For high-school teachers: bringing medical research into the classroom

我是建中畢業的醫學系學生，現在做心血管與營養流行病學研究。我最想帶給高中生的不是艱深的知識，而是一種能力：看到「研究發現⋯⋯」的新聞時，知道該問哪些問題。I am a medical student and a Chien Kuo High School alumnus, now working in cardiovascular and nutritional epidemiology. What I most want to give high-school students is not advanced content but a skill: knowing which questions to ask when a headline says “a study found…”

可以講什麼What I can teach

主打・所有班級Flagship · any class

健康新聞可以信嗎？Can you trust that health headline?

從「喝咖啡會不會傷心臟」這類真實新聞出發，帶學生分辨相關與因果、找出干擾因子與反向因果，並認識不同研究設計各自能回答什麼問題。不需要任何醫學背景。Starting from real headlines such as “does coffee harm the heart?”, students learn to separate correlation from causation, spot confounding and reverse causation, and see what each study design can and cannot answer. No medical background needed.

  • 一至兩節課One or two class periods
  • 不需先備知識No prerequisites
  • 探究與實作・科學論證・媒體識讀Inquiry · scientific argumentation · media literacy

進階・數理資優班與專題課Advanced · science-track and project classes

流行病學與整合生物資訊在醫學研究上的應用Epidemiology and integrative bioinformatics in medical research

從課本的中心法則出發，延伸到基因體、代謝體等多體學，再用實際研究說明：同樣叫超加工食品為什麼有好有壞、「會不會胖」和「胖了會不會生病」是不是同一件事。含手機抽基因活動，全班一起做出多基因性狀的鐘形曲線。From the textbook central dogma to genomics, metabolomics and other omics, then into real studies: why ultra-processed foods are not all alike, and whether “gaining weight” and “getting sick from it” are the same question. Includes a phone-based activity in which the class builds the bell curve of a polygenic trait.

生涯・全年級Careers · all grades

從高中到哈佛：我的醫學生研究之路From high school to Harvard: my research path as a medical student

高中時怎麼開始接觸研究、進大學後如何找題目與指導老師，以及出國做研究的經驗。適合生涯講座，也適合正在規劃自主學習或專題的學生。How I first got into research in high school, how I found questions and mentors at university, and what research abroad is like. Suited to career talks and to students planning independent study or projects.

  • 一節課One class period
  • 不需先備知識No prerequisites
  • 生涯探索・自主學習Career exploration · independent study

What you get

這堂課會留下什麼What the class leaves behind

01

手機互動Phone-based interaction

課堂投票與匿名提問都在學生自己的手機上完成，免登入、不留姓名，台上開哪一題，手機就出現哪一題。Live polls and anonymous questions run on students’ own phones: no login, no names, and each question appears when it is opened on stage.

02

可以重複使用的活動Activities you can reuse

抽基因活動與各單元的探究題，講後會放在演講專區，老師可以直接帶回自己的課堂使用。The gene-draw activity and the inquiry prompts for each unit are posted on the talk page afterwards for you to reuse in your own classes.

03

延伸閱讀Further reading

網誌的「讀懂健康研究的證據思維」與「風險數字怎麼判讀」系列，可以當作課前或課後閱讀。The blog series on evidence thinking and reading risk numbers (in Chinese) work as pre- or post-class reading.

How to invite

怎麼邀請How to invite me

來信時附上下面幾項資訊，我可以比較快回覆適合的安排。Including the following in your email helps me suggest a suitable arrangement quickly.
