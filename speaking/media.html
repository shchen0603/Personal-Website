腹部脂肪、發炎與心臟衰竭Central adiposity, inflammation and heart failure

在近兩千人的追蹤研究中，和心臟衰竭風險有關的是腰圍與發炎指標，不是 BMI；發炎約解釋其中四分之一到三分之一。In a cohort of nearly 2,000 adults, waist-based adiposity and inflammation, not BMI, were associated with heart-failure risk; inflammation explained roughly a quarter to a third of the link.

我的研究My research

誰最能從減鹽獲益Who benefits most from cutting salt

統整 160 篇研究發現，年齡與原本的血壓是判斷鹽敏感性最重要的線索，不同族群的預期效益相差十五倍。Across 160 studies, age and baseline blood pressure were the strongest clues to salt sensitivity, with a fifteen-fold difference in expected benefit between groups.
