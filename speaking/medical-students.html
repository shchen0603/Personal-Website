How to invite

怎麼邀請How to invite me

來信時附上下面幾項資訊，我可以比較快回覆適合的安排。Including the following in your email helps me suggest a suitable arrangement quickly.

  • 主辦單位Organizer 社團、系學會、課程或醫院單位。Student society, department, course or hospital unit.
  • 對象與人數Audience and size 年級、背景，以及大約多少人。Year, background and roughly how many people.
  • 希望的日期與時長Preferred dates and length 有兩三個可行的時段會更容易安排。Two or three possible slots make scheduling much easier.
  • 想要的主題The topic you have in mind 或是學員目前最卡住的地方。Or where participants are currently getting stuck.

我目前仍在醫院見習，可以安排的場次有限，建議盡早來信。實體與線上都可以，講師費依主辦單位的規定即可。I am currently in clinical clerkship, so my availability is limited and early contact helps. In-person and online are both fine, and the speaker fee simply follows the organizer’s standard policy.
